Our Cattle

We are building a foundation herd of miniature Scottish Highland cattle with a clear focus on temperament, structure, and lasting quality — perfect for those seeking mini Highland cows, highland pets, or small-farm livestock in Texas.

Breeding Philosophy

  • Gentle disposition first. Calm, people-oriented miniature Highland cattle are the priority. We want animals that are a pleasure to handle and safe around families — true highland pets as well as productive livestock.
  • Quality over quantity. Our target is a small herd of approximately ten mini Highland cows so that every animal can receive individual attention.
  • Best available genetics. We use artificial insemination to access proven bloodlines and carefully selected sires rather than keeping a bull on site year-round.
  • Heifer focus. We primarily breed for quality heifers that can go on to become the foundation of other small herds of miniature Highland cattle.
  • Polled preference. We aim to produce naturally polled (hornless) mini Highlands while preserving the classic Highland look and long, flowing hair coat.

Round Top Fold is a member of the American Highland Cattle Association. Our official herd prefix is Pending.

At this stage we are still establishing the herd of miniature Highland cattle in Round Top, Texas. No calves are currently available for sale. As the program develops and the first highland calves arrive, we will update this page with information on available animals.

If you are interested in future mini Highland calves or would simply like to learn more about our breeding plans for gentle, quality Highland cattle, please reach out. We are always happy to talk with people who share an appreciation for these remarkable animals.
