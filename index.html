Welcome to Round Top Fold

We raise miniature Highland cattle in the heart of Round Top, Texas — selecting for calm temperament, classic Highland beauty, and the manageable size that makes them ideal for small farms and families.

Located in Fayette County just outside the historic town of Round Top, our fold focuses on quality over quantity. These mini Highland cows and future calves are bred to be docile, people-friendly animals that retain the iconic long hair and gentle eyes of the Scottish Highland breed while remaining easier to handle than full-size cattle.

Whether you are looking for highland pets, small-farm livestock, or simply want to experience the charm of miniature Scottish Highlands in Texas, we invite you to learn more about our breeding program. Visitors are welcome during the renowned Round Top Antique Shows.

Round Top Fold is a member of the American Highland Cattle Association. Our official herd prefix is Pending.

Gentle Disposition

We prioritize calm, people-friendly mini Highland cattle that are a pleasure to handle and ideal as family companions or highland pets.

Quality Genetics

Using artificial insemination and carefully chosen bloodlines to produce consistent, high-quality miniature Highland cattle in Texas.

Polled Preference

We aim for polled (naturally hornless) mini Highlands while preserving the classic long, flowing hair and Highland character.

Visit Us in Round Top

Once established, we will welcome visitors who would like to meet our miniature Highland cattle and see the fold. Perfect for those interested in mini cows, highland calves, or simply experiencing these wonderful animals in person.
